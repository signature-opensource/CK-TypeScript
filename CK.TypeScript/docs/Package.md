The TypeScript configuration of a CKomposable application and the attributes that drive the TypeScript generation (the
engine is CK.TypeScript.Engine): `TypeScriptAspectConfiguration` and `TypeScriptBinPathAspectConfiguration` (the
`<TypeScript>` configurations of a BinPath, their packages and types, their client applications), the TypeScript
packages (`TypeScriptPackage`, `TypeScriptGroup`, `[TypeScriptPackage]`, `[TypeScriptFile]`, `[TypeScriptImportLibrary]`,
`[RegisterTypeScriptType]`, `[AppStyleImport]`, `[YarnPackageExtension]`, `[PackageResolution]`), `[TypeScriptType]`, and the `ClientDirectory` of the client
applications of a host.
See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-TypeScript/blob/stable/CK.TypeScript/README.md).
